# 239a. BUG-045 (S1): generated plans make plain pages. Make the plan visual-first and make the desk elevate Grok, never restrict it

## WHY (read this first)

We are building a harness that automatically brings the best features of the world's best websites to Grok, so people never need to understand complex languages or tools. An owner answers one simple interview in plain words; the desk turns that into art direction, a brand kit, generated images and video, award-level motion and 3D, and the expert prompt language Grok needs, and then proves the result with screenshots. The success bar is not "better than plain Grok". It is orders of magnitude better than plain Grok given the same brief, and at least on par with aura.build. Every item below exists to serve that bar; anything in the plan that makes Grok's output plainer is a bug.

Creative framing (applies to every item): the design library, golden templates, technique notes and reference snippets are inspiration. They teach Grok the language of what is possible: technique names, prompt vocabulary and small working snippets. Grok must remix, improve and add its own creativity on every page. It never copies a template or a reference wholesale and is never pigeonholed into one look, one library or one technique. Originality counts as much as richness (see the benchmark in 239b item 7). Images and video come from the user's existing Grok login and subscription at no additional cost (see Imagine transport), generated automatically: no budget cap, no price tiers, no confirm step.

## Principle (read before any item)

The desk exists to make Grok's output far more beautiful than Grok alone, not less. Grok is the designer and the engineer. The harness gives it rich context: art direction, a design language with named techniques, reference examples, generated assets, and screenshots of its own work. The harness enforces only what must hold: honest copy (no invented facts, prices, testimonials or people), accessibility (reduced motion stays complete, contrast, keyboard), phone performance budgets, licenses (credit line and license text preserved for any open-source code borrowed), and the project folder. There is no spend cap, price tier or generation confirm: images and video are generated automatically on the user's existing Grok login and subscription at no additional cost (see Imagine transport). Any appropriate library may be used: GSAP, Three.js or React Three Fiber, Motion, Lenis, Astro, vanilla JS, Howler and others. Everything else is guidance and vocabulary, never a ban. A generated plan must never contain a blanket ban on effect libraries, a "leave a TODO" for imagery, or a cap that stops a visual pass from adding something new. Library entries and templates are references to remix, never a form to fill in.
