# 239b. BUG-045 part 2: the design library, the brand kit, video and sound, and a benchmark the desk must win

## WHY (read this first)

We are building a harness that automatically brings the best features of the world's best websites to Grok, so people never need to understand complex languages or tools. An owner answers one simple interview in plain words; the desk turns that into art direction, a brand kit, generated images and video, award-level motion and 3D, and the expert prompt language Grok needs, and then proves the result with screenshots. The success bar is not "better than plain Grok". It is orders of magnitude better than plain Grok given the same brief, and at least on par with aura.build. Every item below exists to serve that bar; anything in the plan that makes Grok's output plainer is a bug.

Creative framing (applies to every item): library entries, technique notes and snippets are inspiration. They teach Grok the language of what is possible: technique names, prompt vocabulary and small working snippets. Grok must remix, improve and add its own creativity on every page, never copy a template or a reference wholesale and never be pigeonholed into one look, one library or one technique. The benchmark rewards originality as well as richness. Images and video come from the user's existing Grok login and subscription at no additional cost (see Imagine transport), generated automatically with no budget cap and no confirm.
