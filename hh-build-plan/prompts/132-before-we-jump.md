## Steps

1. Derive hosting and required gaps from answers.

2. Unapproved sections become questions that name the section.

3. Test a fully approved, fully answered project with a legal page. Expect exactly 3 questions from the filler or the single launch question plus fillers to reach 3. Pick one behavior and test it: at least 3, at most 8.

4. Test hosting `no idea` produces a question containing `host`.

5. No exclamation marks.

6. Do not include a question that asks for a testimonial.

7. Export the function.

- Empty approvals object treats every known section as unapproved. Use BRAND_SECTIONS.
