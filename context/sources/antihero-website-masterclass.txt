BEFORE YOU START




  IF YOU CAN TALK,
  YOU CAN BUILD
This guide takes you from nothing to a live, premium website. You will not write code. You
will describe what you want, show your AI examples, and correct it until the site looks right.

Work through the steps in order. Each one ends with something done. The copy-paste
prompts are in the grey boxes. Swap in your own business, fonts, and files.

It costs nothing to start. You do not need a domain or hosting until the last step, and an old
computer is fine, because most of the work happens in the cloud.

WHAT YOU NEED


ANY COMPUTER               A 2017 machine works. Speed does not matter much.

GOOGLE ANTIGRAVITY         Your AI coding workspace. It has a free version.

GEMINI                     The model you build with inside AntiGravity.

NOTEBOOKLM                 Free research tool from Google.

GROK                       For the hero video. Runway or Midjourney also work.

HOSTINGER                  Domain and hosting, only when you are ready to go live.

    STEP 01




 GIVE YOUR
 AI APPS
Your phone has apps that make it more useful. MCP (Model Context Protocol) does
the same for your AI. Think of your AI as a brilliant brain in a glass jar. MCP gives it
hands.


1       Start with the official list. Browse the MCP servers repository and the Figma MCP (links on the Links
        and Resources page). Pick only what helps you build a website.

2       Find skills. Search X for "AntiGravity Skills". Skills are add-ons that make AntiGravity better at specific
        jobs.

3       Let your AI install them. Paste the link or list into AntiGravity and ask it to install everything for you.
        Talk to it out loud if typing slows you down.

4       Run the safety check first, every time. Some packages hide bad instructions. Paste this before any
        install:
            I want to install the MCP repository from this link. Before I do, please read through the
            code, check it for security vulnerabilities, verify its reputation score, and tell me if
            it is completely safe to install.



5       Treat it like a teammate. Say please and thank you, and talk to it like a friend. The answers get better
        when the conversation is clear and calm.




     “MCP just connects it to stuff.”
     RULE    Never install random scripts blindly. If you did not run the safety check, do not install it.

    STEP 02




 RESEARCH
 FIRST
Every AI likes to be talked to differently. Before you build, have NotebookLM
research your niche and write the exact prompts for your coding AI.


1          Open NotebookLM and choose deep research. Not fast research. Deep research pulls from many
           sources at once and costs nothing.

2          Paste the research prompt. Change the business to yours:
            I am building a website for a high-end physical construction company. Research the best,
            fastest, and most modern programming stack to build this today. Create a comprehensive,
            step-by-step website architecture plan. Then, write out the exact, highly-detailed
            prompts I need to copy and paste into my coding AI (Google AntiGravity) to execute it.
            Ensure the entire structure is fully optimized for both traditional Google SEO and modern
            AI Search Engines (Siri, ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.).



3          Ask for a prompting report. Create a report on how to prompt your model for your landing page, for
           example: how to prompt Gemini to build a stunning landing page for your project, with a video on the
           splash page.

4          Keep the report. You will paste it into AntiGravity as context in Step 5. You do not need to understand
           every line of it.

5          Use it for anything else you need to learn. NotebookLM also makes slide decks, mind maps,
           flashcards, and video overviews.




     “So they like to be talked to differently.”
     WHY  Plan for AI search from day one. The research prompt asks for Google SEO and AI search engines
     together.

    STEP 03




 FIND WHAT
 YOU LOVE
You do not need to be a designer. You need to be good at finding sources and
showing your AI exactly what you like. That is context engineering.


1        Browse the galleries. Aura Build, Awwwards (strong on GSAP motion), Behance, Godly, Lapa Ninja, and
         Siteinspire.

2        Pick 3 to 5 sites. Not 20. Too much context confuses the model, the same way 30 instructions would
         confuse a person.

3        Screenshot the sections you like. Put every screenshot in one folder, with a note on each: what you
         like and why. Links help too, because your AI can read a site's code.

4        Choose 2 fonts. One for headers from Fontshare, one for body text from Google Fonts. Font Squirrel and
         Typewolf are good for browsing.

5        Drag the folder into AntiGravity and paste:
          I have attached visual references of websites I absolutely love. I want you to build me a
          site with a similar premium vibe, structure, and aesthetic, but make it completely unique
          to my brand. Use [Insert Fontshare Font Name] for all the big headers and [Insert Google
          Font Name] for the body text.




     “You just need to find websites that you really love.”
     CREDIT   If you build off someone's design, credit them at the bottom of your site with a link. You do not need to
     steal anything.

    STEP 04




 SPEAK THE
 STACK
You do not need to know what these tools do. Naming them in your prompt gets you
a more polished, faster site.


1       Learn the words. Astro is the framework. Vanilla JS handles logic. Node.js runs it. GSAP animates.
        Lenis smooths scrolling. Framer Motion brings elements in nicely. Three.js renders 3D, often with GLB
        model files.

2       Load the stack in your first build prompt.
            Build this website using Astro for the framework and Vanilla JS for the logic. I want the
            site to feel incredibly premium and expensive, so you MUST use GSAP for all entrance
            animations and Lenis for buttery-smooth scrolling.



3       Add 3D only if it fits your brand. Attach a GLB model, or ask your AI to find open-source GLB models
        online, then paste:
            Embed the attached GLB model on the page using Three.js. Make the 3D object float gently
            and rotate slowly based on the user's mouse movement across the screen.




     “You're gonna get a more polished project”
     NOTE    3D is heavier to run. Start without it if you want the first build to go smoothly.

    STEP 05




 BUILD THE
 HERO FIRST
Do not build the whole site in one prompt. Build the hero section, get it right, then
move to the next section.


1          Make a background video. Take a photo of your work. Ask AntiGravity to write a Grok prompt that turns
           it into a dramatic, realistic video loop, and say what it is for and why. Generate it in Grok (Runway or
           Midjourney also work) and download it.

2          Open a fresh chat. Attach your research report, reference folder, fonts, and the video. If a file will not
           attach, right-click it, choose Copy as path, and paste the path.

3          Say the emotion. Tell it what visitors should feel: quality, trust, calm. Tell it the business name and
           location too, or it will make one up.

4          Ask for the hero only, plus navigation.
            Use this video file as the looping background for the hero section.

           Add: build a menu, no AI slop, keep it contemporary and elegant, and make it good enough for
           Awwwards.

5          Perfect it before moving on. Then ask for the next section, and the next. Remind it to keep using the
           earlier context.




     “You just wanna do it in small sections.”
     WHY    Overloading the AI is the most common mistake. Small asks get better results.

    STEP 06




 SEE IT.
 FIX IT.
The first pass will be close and a little wrong. Show your AI what is wrong, ask
plainly, and reload.


1       Preview it. Ask AntiGravity to start the development server and open the page, and to double-check
        that it is its best work first.

2       Know what localhost means. The site is running only on your computer. Anyone you send that link to
        will not be able to open it.

3       Fix with screenshots. Screenshot the problem, drag it into the chat, and describe it: overlapping
        words, wrong name, a photo you do not like. Ask for 1 or 2 changes at a time.

4       Reload properly. Hold Shift and reload to see the latest version.


5       Make it mobile. Mobile is not optional. Paste:
          Review the code you just wrote. Make sure this entire website is perfectly responsive and
          optimized for mobile devices (iOS and Android). Ensure all GSAP animations and Lenis
          scrolling effects perform flawlessly on a phone screen without causing lag or breaking
          the layout.




     “It doesn't have to be perfect.”
     IF IT GETS CONFUSED    Too much context can make it repeat itself. Remove some, start a new chat with the
     same prompt, and go one item at a time.

    STEP 07




 THE POLISH
 LAYER
Small effects make a site feel premium. Ask for them, look at the result, then ask
for less.


1       Add white space. Ask for substantially more white space. It is the fastest upgrade there is.


2       Blend the video. Ask for a smooth fade from the hero video into the next section with no visible line. If
        the fade covers your text, make it shorter.

3       Add motion. A marquee scroller, a video driven by scrolling, headlines that come in from different
        angles as you scroll, and a horizontal GSAP scroll.

4       Add hover glow and texture. A warm, slow WebGL glow on titles when the mouse passes over them.
        Subtle background tracers. A light noise overlay, then tone it down or remove it if it is too much.

5       Steal an effect, politely. Paste the page link and say: on this website, I like this section, I want mine to
        move like this.

6       Add optional sound. Download a track from Pixabay Music, then paste:
         Add an elegant, minimal play button in the bottom corner of the site that triggers this
         ambient audio track. It MUST start paused by default so it doesn't annoy the user, but it
         should fade in smoothly when they click play.




     “These are small effects.”
     DIAL BACK   If an effect draws attention to itself, it is too strong. Ask your AI to halve it.

    STEP 08




 BLOG AND
 SCHEMA
A beautiful website is nothing if nobody sees it. A blog brings people in, and
Schema markup helps search engines and AI bots understand every page.


1       Add the blog.
         Add a highly SEO-optimized blog directory to the site so I can drive organic traffic.
         Ensure every single article page uses standard Schema.org markup so Google's web crawlers
         and modern AI bots understand the content perfectly and rank it higher.



2       Use your research. Your NotebookLM report from Step 2 already covers Google SEO and AI search.
        Paste the relevant part in with the prompt.

3       Say it upfront next time. If you forgot to ask for SEO in the first build, add it now. On your next site, put
        "SEO optimized" in the first prompt.

4       Check the markup. Schema.org is the standard vocabulary your AI uses. Ask it to confirm every article
        page has it.




     “A beautiful website is nothing if nobody sees it.”
     THINK OF IT THIS WAY     A site with no traffic is a digital billboard in the middle of the desert.

    STEP 09




 GO
 LIVE
Build locally until you are happy. Then get a domain and hosting and let your AI
deploy the site for you.


1       Set up Hostinger. Create an account (you can sign in with Google), choose a hosting plan, then go to
        Domains and get a new domain.

2       Create an API token. In hPanel, open Dev Tools, then API, then Generate Token. Give it a name and an
        expiry, click Generate, and copy it immediately.

3       Store the token safely. Hostinger shows it only once. Treat it like a password and never post it
        anywhere.

4       Deploy.
         I am ready to deploy this website to the internet. Here is my Hostinger API Token: [Paste
         Your Token Here]. Please use the Hostinger API to package up this local website and
         deploy it directly to my live server.



5       Check the live site on your phone. Then share the real link, not localhost.




     “You can literally just get a domain.”
     SECURITY   If a token leaks, delete it in hPanel and generate a new one.
