# Award sites

Look at what a reference is doing. Name the job of the signature moment, how scroll carries one idea, and what the phone still shows. Take the thinking. Do not clone the skin, the type, the palette, or the assets.
Source: context/research/03-awwwards-anatomy.md
Source: hh-build-plan/RESEARCH-ADDENDUM.md gallery section.

## What to notice

Look for one signature moment, scroll used as a timeline, type used as an image, a preloader that is part of the intro on a heavy WebGL page, a real phone fallback, and sound that stays off until the visitor opts in. These are ways to read a site. They are not a kit to copy.
Source: hh-build-plan/RESEARCH-ADDENDUM.md gallery section.

Do not repeat older tag counts. This pack did not re-count them.
Source: hh-build-plan/RESEARCH-ADDENDUM.md gallery section.

Do not state that Site of the Year is announced in February. The addendum says that rule is not from Awwwards.
Source: hh-build-plan/RESEARCH-ADDENDUM.md gallery section.

The Awwwards rubric weights design 40%, usability 30%, creativity 20%, and content 10%. Those weights are a jury rubric, not a conversion claim.
