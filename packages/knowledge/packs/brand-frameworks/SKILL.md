# Brand frameworks

Read `BRAND.md` and `VOICE.md`. This pack holds no client's brand. If a field is missing, mark it TODO. Do not fill the gap with a guess.

## Purpose

Read purpose from the Purpose section of `BRAND.md`. It says why the brand exists for a real person. Do not replace it with a line about revenue. Do not skip it and start from a logo, a color, or a headline.

## Positioning

Read positioning from the Positioning section of `BRAND.md`. Use that line. Do not add a visitor, a benefit, a rival, or a proof the section does not contain. A bolder line is allowed only when `BRAND.md` already has one.

## Proof

Proof is a fact already written in `BRAND.md`. Suggesting a place to put proof is allowed. Inventing it is not. Do not invent awards, testimonials, customer quotes, logo strips, star ratings, customer counts, or press hits. If the file has no award, the site does not mention awards. A testimonial block with no quote in the file is an invention. Leave it out.

## Voice

Read voice from `VOICE.md` and from the Voice section of `BRAND.md`. Traits, vocabulary, punctuation, and extra bans live there. Use that voice. Do not invent a second voice in the prompt. Site copy still follows the anti-slop pack. `VOICE.md` may add bans. It may not license a banned word in generated site copy.

## Stance

Use the archetype `BRAND.md` already names. One primary stance is enough. Do not pile on personalities the interview did not choose.
