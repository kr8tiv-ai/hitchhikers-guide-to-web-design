# Copywriting

Read `VOICE.md` and `BRAND.md` before writing. Lines come from those files and from facts the client already gave. This pack does not supply a client's lines. Apply the anti-slop pack to every line, including headings and buttons.

## One idea

Each section carries one idea, and the section plan names it. The headline states that idea. The body supports it. A second idea waits for the next section. Do not repeat the same promise in three cards.

## Buttons

Buttons are verbs. They name the action and the outcome the visitor gets. Read the button samples in `VOICE.md` when they exist. Do not use Learn More, Get Started, or Submit.

## 404

The 404 page is calm. Read the 404 sample in `VOICE.md` when it exists. If it does not, write one quiet sentence that the page is missing and one real next step that the site already has, then stop. No joke that needs a raised voice. No invented product. No exclamation marks.

## Facts

A headline is short and specific. A caption names what is in the picture. A page ends on one action. If a number, a name, or a quote is missing, mark it TODO. Do not invent it to make the line sound finished.

## Punctuation

No exclamation marks. No em dashes, unless `VOICE.md` overrides punctuation. Empty states and form errors use the same voice as the rest of the site, and the samples in `VOICE.md` when they exist.
