# Awwwards breakdowns

Links and our notes. Ten public sites from late 2025 through October 2026. We did not copy their pages. A score below is the public figure on the linked award page, checked when that page stated one. Use the pattern, then make a different picture.

## Format

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/format
Public record: Site of the Day, 19 October 2026, Obys with DES.
Our note: The subject is the frame itself. A useful borrow is a section whose crop, ratio, or margin is the idea, changed on scroll, with the work still readable if the crop never moves.

## Signal-A Studio

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/signal-a-studio
Public record: Site of the Day, 18 October 2026, Signal-A and Maciej Zuk.
Our note: A studio site that wins on craft density. Borrow the discipline of one interface system, type, grid, and motion, held across every case, rather than a new trick per project.

## Belgrade Arbor

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/belgrade-arbor
Public record: Site of the Day, 17 October 2026, score 7.22, developer award 7.33, Square43 Studio.
Our note: Architecture told as a sequence of spaces. Borrow a chapter per room, a camera that arrives with the chapter, and a material that takes the building's color into the glass. The house itself stays theirs.

## OFFFForum

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/offfforum
Public record: Site of the Day, 16 October 2026, REF Digital.
Our note: A festival has a calendar, a lineup, and a place. Borrow a scroll that turns the program into a timed sequence, and keep a plain list for someone who only needs the dates.

## Overworld Audio

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/overworld-audio
Public record: Site of the Day, 15 October 2026, score 7.38, developer award 7.36, Rogier de Boeve.
Our note: The portfolio is a map. Borrow the idea that navigation can be a place, with sound or a still as the proof at each point, and a direct index beside the map so the work is never only a discovery puzzle.

## Lidar Drone Scanning

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lidar-drone-scanning
Public record: Site of the Day, 6 October 2026, Riotters.
Our note: A point cloud of a real scan, drawn live. Borrow the restraint of one dataset and one camera, and a still of the scan when the canvas cannot run. The scan is the content, not a texture on a generic hero.

## EDOLUS

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/edolus
Public record: Site of the Day, 4 October 2026, score 7.2, Yann Trevelot / Vertex3D.
Our note: A dark field and a cinematic move through infrastructure. Borrow the exposure, the slow camera, and the decision to let one object occupy the frame. Copying the machines would miss the point.

## Why Zero

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/why-zero
Public record: Site of the Month, September 2026, Sindhur Dutta.
Our note: An argument about a school, carried as an immersive story. Borrow a thesis the scroll can prove, with a quiet reading path beside the set piece.

## Warm and Fuzzy

Link: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/warm-fuzzy
Public record: Site of the Day, 12 September 2026, score 7.26, developer award 7.52.
Our note: A small company site with a strong palette and a hover that shows the work. Borrow color that is actually the brand, and a reveal that stays on the work instead of on the chrome. The developer score is the reminder that the motion has to stay fast.

## Lando Norris

Link: https://landonorris.com/
Public record: Site of the Year 2025, OFF+BRAND, noted in our research at context/research/03-awwwards-anatomy.md. The year index is https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/.
Our note: A driver site that mixes a long scroll film with WebGL. Borrow the pacing, one signature run down the page, and the fact that the name and the results stay in the document. The likeness and the racing imagery belong to that site.

Source: award dates and scores are the public figures on the links above. The notes are ours.
