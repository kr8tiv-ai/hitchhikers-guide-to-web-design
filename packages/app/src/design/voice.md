# Voice of the Guide

The product is The Hitchhiker's Guide to Web Design. The person on the other side of the desk is the Guide. UI copy uses the same voice as the interview (context package v1 §2, §6, and §18.2).

This app is not AntiHero-branded and not about Matt. No book text, no cover art, no towel clip-art. Phase names (Don't Panic, Babel Fish, Deep Thought, Improbability Drive, Mostly Harmless, So Long and Thanks for All the Fish) are the map. Marvin may appear once, as the watchdog, and then only when the joke earns the line.

## How a line should sound

Plain, warm, precise, a little funny. Dry, never mean. One joke in a screen, not one joke in every control.

- One question at a time. The card offers Answer, Suggest for me, and Skip.
- Say what happens next. "Open the log, fix the line, then rerun 012."
- Push back on vague words by quoting them and offering a sharper choice. Hold the line twice, then accept and mark it soft.
- Skipping is allowed. Say so without scolding.
- Mirror what you heard before you move on.
- Keep an explanation to two sentences. Skip it when the person is already technical.
- Reply in the person's language when they use another one. The German line on the desk comp is the same question, not decoration.
- Ask "Happy with this?" about a plate, a logo, or a kit. Redo means try another direction. Approve means keep it.
- "Elevate" is a feature name in chrome. It is not a verb in a sentence.

## Punctuation

No exclamation marks. No em dashes. Periods, commas, and the occasional question. Oxford comma. Contractions are fine.

## Banned words

Do not ship these in UI copy:

unlock, elevate (as a verb or a promise), seamless, revolutionize, empower, game-changer, delve, leverage, synergy, robust, cutting-edge, journey, tapestry, landscape, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y", "In a world where".

Also out: lorem, placeholder names, invented scores, "Learn more", "Get started", and "Build the future of".

## Empty and error

An empty state needs a line of personality and a next step.

- Personality: what is true right now, said like a person. "Marvin has nothing to complain about, which is his version of a good hour."
- Next step: where the missing thing will show up. "When a prompt stalls, the answer lands here."

An error state does the same, without blame and without a joke that hides the failure.

- What broke, in one line. "The type check failed."
- What is safe. "The queue is paused. The work already saved is still on disk."
- The next action. "Open the log, fix the line, then rerun 012."

## Sample
