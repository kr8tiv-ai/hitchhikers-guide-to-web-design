# Don't Panic. A pitch, one page.

The Hitchhiker's Guide to Web Design is a local, free, spec-driven desk that interviews you like a brand agency and then builds the site with Grok. It is not a vending machine for purple gradients. You answer questions. It writes a brief, a brand, and a stack of prompts. You say yes before any of that is allowed to build.

## Why it is useful

Most AI site builders skip the part where someone asks what the site is for. This one starts there. Suggest for me is available on every question, and a suggestion stays marked as an assumption until you keep it. Approvals are a real step: the brief, the brand, and every build prompt wait for a yes. The work stays on your machine, in files you can read.

A stranger can see that in two minutes, before they decide whether to stay.

## Try it in two minutes

You need Node.js 22 or newer. From a fresh clone:

```
node scripts/start.mjs
```

That command installs the workspace with corepack and opens the desk. On the empty desk, choose Open the sample. Mostly Towels is a short project file in the same format as a real save. The first-five-minutes tour walks the desk, the sample, the brief, Suggest, the approval step, and where saves live. Skip tour puts it away. Replay tour, in the menu, brings it back.

If something is missing, `pnpm exec hh doctor` says what it means, why it matters, and the fix for your operating system.
